Racing Line Challenge

Human vs. barely-AI
How it works.
  1. Tap the tarmac to drop numbered markers. The car starts at the chequered line, heading up, and drives them in order.
  2. Each marker has a target speed. Select one and change it. Too fast and the kart slides off; too slow and you lose time.
  3. Hit Try. Real car physics (the same kart as my AI) decides what happens. Finish a lap to get a time and a code to share.

Your line as a code

A code is just your markers and speeds, so anyone can paste it and get exactly your lap time. Post yours in the comments.

How honest is the physics?

The kart is a line-by-line port of the car model in my C# neural-network driving app: 158 kg, 3.5 hp, rear-wheel drive, slip-angle tyres (Pacejka), weight transfer, drag and a steering lock that shrinks with speed. The track mask, the car's size and the collision points come from that app, so a lap here is comparable to a lap there.

Your "driver" is a plain pure-pursuit controller: it steers towards a point a little way along your line and uses throttle and brake to chase the speed you asked for. It will not rescue a bad line. It will not get lucky either: the same code always gives the same lap.

You leave the track the moment any of the kart's nine collision points touches grass.

Built as a Sunday experiment. The AI it is racing has 5 lidar beams and a tiny network.
